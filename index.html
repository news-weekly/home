The News Weekly

Our neighborhood's weekly newspaper

The News Weekly began with a simple dream: a young elementary school student in Downingtown, Pennsylvania, wanted to create his own newspaper to share science stories, fun facts, puzzles, recipes, and other fascinating ideas with his neighbors and teachers. Every week, he writes, designs, and publishes the entire paper all on his own. Then, rain or shine, he hops on his bike and rides around the community, delivering each issue by hand.
